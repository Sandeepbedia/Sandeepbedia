### 🎵 AB Bravo — Music Player for Android
A feature-rich, modern Android music player built with **Kotlin + Jetpack Compose**.

- 🎧 Streams from **JioSaavn & YouTube Music**
- 📥 Downloads & offline playback
- ⏱ Real listening stats & playback records
- 🎨 Neumorphic UI, dark/light themes, lyrics
- 📜 Licensed under **GPL-3.0**
